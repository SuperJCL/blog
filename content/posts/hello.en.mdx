## About this site

The site runs on the Next.js App Router. Every page lives under `app/[lang]/`, so English and Chinese share the same pages:

```ts
export const locales = ["en", "zh"] as const;
```

When a post has a translation, the page links to the other language. This one has a [Chinese version](/zh/blog/hello).
