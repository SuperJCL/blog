<Problem>

A city-level public-safety technology competition asked teams to find target records among the city's millions of capture and alert entries, quickly and accurately, by combining visual operators, models and conditions.

</Problem>

<Role>

Core frontend developer. I built the interaction logic for composing operators and running searches, and in this cross-team project I also adjusted the SQL and helped optimize the queries.

</Role>

<Approach>

### Search conditions as composable operators

I implemented the interaction logic for composing operators and running searches: operators, models and conditions combine freely in the interface, and a finished combination runs as a search.

### Optimizing queries across teams

The work was split across departments: the big-data team owned the intelligent-analysis APIs, and I built the frontend for composing operators and running searches, which also meant adjusting the SQL. It was my first production SQL. Together with the big-data team, I tracked down slow queries and optimized searches over millions of rows.

</Approach>
