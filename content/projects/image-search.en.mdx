<Problem>

Campus clients needed a product they could run on their own: upload a photo, search people and vehicle captures by their features, plot the matches on a map, and archive the results and trajectories.

</Problem>

<Role>

One of the main developers. On this project I moved from frontend to full stack: about half of the Nest.js backend APIs and their docs, the archive module's frontend, and the product's packaging and installation.

</Role>

<Approach>

### Backend APIs

I wrote about half of the backend APIs in Nest.js and kept their documentation up to date.

### Archive module

I built the frontend of the archive module, where search results and trajectories are filed.

The product had to install on its own, so everything from build to install was automated: Jenkins builds, Docker images, and install, upgrade and uninstall scripts.
